In a crossover design, each of 30 subjects received all 5 treatments (VEHICLE, LOW, HIGH, NOMINAL, OTHER), one per week, over 5 randomized weeks. Fixed weeks were identical for all subjects (MAX in week 6). Randomization was stratified by sex. Treatment sequences were generated by constrained randomization with LIBRA 1.0.0 (Brejtr et al., 2026): an integer linear program (solved with HiGHS) selected sequences that optimize balance, with the criteria optimized one at a time in order of priority: each treatment occurring equally often in every week within every stratum; then each treatment being directly preceded by every other treatment equally often (first-order carry-over balance); then subjects sharing a treatment in one week receiving different treatments in the next. The final design achieved ideal week balance, carry-over balance and spread within each week change. One of the equally balanced designs was chosen at random by a random walk among them; the choice was random but not uniform, because listing them did not finish within the time limit (seed 2026); subjects were randomly assigned to sequences within strata and treatment labels were randomly permuted. The search was limited by solver work rather than by time, so the same settings and seed reproduce the schedule exactly.
